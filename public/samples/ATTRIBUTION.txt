The upstream collection prepared the samples for consistent playback. EarTrainer
uses the existing files with sampler pitch transposition and schedules timing,
volume and duration. The guitar-strum feature does not modify the audio files.
This is a sample-based simulation, without direction-specific strum recordings,
velocity layers or palm-mute articulations. Attribution does not imply endorsement.
